Who is responsible

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Fonts are served from this website, not from a third party.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, browser and the time of the request, to deliver the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Booking a call

The “Book a call” buttons take you to my booking page on Cal.com (Cal.com, Inc.). If you book, Cal.com processes the name, email address and answers you enter, adds the meeting to my Google Calendar (Google Ireland Ltd) and creates a Google Meet link. I use them only to hold the call and follow up (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Cal.com's and Google's privacy policies apply to that processing.

The newsletter sign-up box is provided by Substack, Inc. It loads from Substack only on pages that show it. If you subscribe, Substack processes your email address to send you the newsletter, based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe from any issue. Substack's privacy policy applies.

When you email me

If you contact me by email, I use your details only to reply and to discuss working together (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). I keep them only as long as needed for that, or as the law requires.

Your rights
