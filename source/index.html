About

I go where things are being built.

I trained in the big machine: IBM, Credit Suisse and Lloyds Banking Group, where I helped create TSB Bank. Then I jumped to start-ups and have been building sales engines ever since, at Pronto, Wheely, Arbonics and UXCam.

Along the way I founded my own company, Courage Global, with a co-founder. We spent months perfecting the website, the brand and the concept, and far too little time in front of customers. It didn't work. The lesson stuck: the idea comes from you, but the market tells you if it works. That's why I start with buyers, not slides.

Beyond work: proud dad of a one-year-old, building our life in Berlin. Eight marathons, kayaking in Antarctica, the world's first ice obstacle course in the Arctic Circle, and six years in intelligence in the British Army Reserve.
