What I believe

Agents do the track. People own the stations.

01

You can't automate everything

AI can do a lot of the work. It can't replace the people at the top who understand the market, the culture, the customer and the nuance in a message. Strategy stays human. The agents carry it out.

02

Teach it like a new hire

Nobody expects a new rep to be great on day one. Agents are the same: give them your best examples, correct them, talk to them, and they get better. Skip that, and you'll never get the output you need.

03

Test, learn, repeat

Treat AI like a lean startup. Small tests, real data, quick changes, and nothing scaled until it works. It's a constant learning process, not a one-off install.

Builds

What I've built, and what's running now.

Live · UXCam, 2026

An inbound agent that replies in five minutes

Built with HubSpot and Claude Code, with Apollo, Lusha and LinkedIn Sales Navigator for enrichment. Several agents in one flow: one enriching every lead, one scoring fit from the profile, one writing the message. Each new lead landed in a Slack channel with the qualification, the details that mattered and a reply already drafted. The team could check it, give feedback right there on the message, and approve to send, so every piece of feedback was collected in one place.

First reply went from about a day to 5 minutes.

Live · UXCam, 2026

An outbound engine built on public data

Clay and Claude Code, using app store data to find companies where the product could make a big difference, with Lemlist warming up the sending addresses. Then a lot of testing on the message by person, company and timing.

The first run reached about 500 accounts and 4,000 contacts in the first month.

Live · this site, 2026

A lead engine on free tools

The scorecard on this site sends a report from my own inbox through a small Google Apps Script, records the lead in HubSpot and offers a call through Cal.com, which logs the meeting too. It cost nothing to run, and the report lands in the inbox, not spam.

The full build is in the free Workflows pack below.
