Speaker bio

For your programme.

Harry James Coburn is a Berlin-based commercial strategy and go-to-market adviser for growing tech companies. He has led sales at UXCam, Arbonics, Wheely and Pronto, companies backed by Headline, Plural, Concentric, Seedcamp and Playfair, and trained at IBM, Credit Suisse and Lloyds Banking Group. His work centres on one idea: find the commercial formula first, then use AI to scale it.
