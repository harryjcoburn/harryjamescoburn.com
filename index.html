02 / The method

The Commercial Engine

Strategy is easy to write down. The hard part is getting a team to run it on Monday morning. Every engagement follows three steps.

i.

Diagnose

Find where revenue is really lost: the customer you target, the offer, the funnel, the team and the data.

ii.

Design

Who to sell to, what to say, the process, the numbers and the AI workflows that make it fast.

iii.

Build

Put it into the CRM, the team's week and the forecast. Then hand it over so it runs without me.

03 / The work

Four builds. One pattern.

Zero to one, two scale-ups and a rebuild. Each time: find the right customer, build the system, get the team running it, and the growth follows.

The rebuild · 2026

UXCam

AI product analytics SaaS, ~$5M ARR. Inbound was saturating, the ICP was unclear, and a new AI agent product was about to launch.

  • Reset the ICP and the story for the AI launch
  • Built outbound from zero with Clay and AI-personalised sequences
  • AI agents on inbound: replies in 5 minutes, not a day
Inbound conversion ~25% → ~40%
The scale · 2024–25

Arbonics

Forest carbon start-up with no effective sales team and a broken CRM, selling a complex product to landowners. About €10M in estimated contract value won during my time there.

  • Reset the ICP to larger landowners and enterprise
  • Built the team from 2 to 12 across five markets; launched Lithuania and Sweden
  • Average sales cycle cut from 75 to 16 days
Sales up 37× in nine months
