All logos are trademarks of their respective owners. They are used on harryjamescoburn.com only to say where Harry has worked or who backed companies he worked at. Each was retrieved on 2026-10-12 with a browser and copied from the SVG markup as served. Colours are unchanged unless a note says otherwise.
